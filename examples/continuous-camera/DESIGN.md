# Camera motion proof: host-owned visual shell

Mode: Experience. The owner approved the fictional camera's seven-beat continuous story; warm editorial defaults are used pending visual feedback. This is a representative integrated preview, not an approved production site.

The object is the visual lead. An ivory photography-publication surface, Bodoni Moda display lettering, graphite housing and brass accents connect the exterior and interior inspection. Navigation, chapter copy, typographic scale and final action belong to this shell. The separately packaged motion runtime receives only its mount point and authored data; it does not reset these choices.

Desktop keeps copy in the left third and a persistent stage on the right. Mobile keeps the product above readable text, with shorter vertical stage allocation. Native scrolling progresses the authored beats; optional review controls expose exact poses. No global scroll override, external media, paid generation, fabricated product specifications or enquiry backend.

Font: [Bodoni Moda from Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bodonimoda), shipped with its OFL license. Every geometric part is an original conceptual proxy. Owner creative acceptance is pending.
